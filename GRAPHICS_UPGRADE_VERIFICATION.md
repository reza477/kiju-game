# Full graphics upgrade — prototype 0.9

This visual cycle follows the owner's request to improve every aspect of the graphics as far as possible. The fixed independent whole-game art rubric and four-round limit remain in force. Previous cycles and their scores are unchanged.

## Design constraints

- Gothic districts still form one upward stack. Upgrades lift higher districts; harness work adds capacity only.
- The cyborg castle remains half-height relative to its flesh counterpart. The robot, body weapons and backpack footprint retain their established dimensions.
- All six carrier identities, faction architecture and clothing, existing saves and local play remain supported.
- Game assets and rendering stay local; the existing private GitHub repository stores the source.

## First builder attempt

The renderer adds a scene-linear four-scale highlight bloom, clearer indirect lighting, layered cloud shading, inward metal bevels, and physical cloth/glass/paint response. High mode renders at least 1.25 times display resolution; balanced and performance controls remain available. Combat receives textured muzzle flashes, local muzzle lighting, sparks, fragments, impact smoke and missile exhaust. Presentation uses a quieter HUD frame and stronger title-selection contrast.

Environment work adds distinct oak/aspen/pine habits, coherent forest belts and understory, exposed slate, gravel spits and a broken railway viaduct. Ground colour now follows woodland/biome structure with metre-scale stone UVs and wet bank transitions. The river has deeper channels, shallow bands and restrained current detail. Deer, birds and butterflies gain clearer forms. Existing terrain heights, saved scenery identities, resource protection and the 57-animal population remain intact.

City work adds chamfered crawler hulls, vaulted industrial undercrofts, a swept airship keel/navigation bay, more convincing propellers and balloon panels, and stronger Gothic chapter/crown forms. Castle rendering and obstruction still use the same solids. Character work adds continuous anatomical shaping and muscle flex, an articulated breathing jaw, gaze/blinks and independent fingers to the flesh titan; cyborg plate seams and fasteners; and resident fingers, expressions, faction ornaments and independently deforming cloth.

Visual quality is judged from the actual game, not from passing tests or implementation size.

## Second builder attempt

The ranked larger-form issues drive this attempt. Landscape transitions use slope, concavity and river wetness to replace broad saturated lawn patches with connected soil/grass cover. Existing grass inventory is reused: 677 free tufts relocated, 10,037 shaped, zero new instances. Castle galleries and upper stages recess toward the protected walking envelope; fewer repeated side windows, larger chapter openings, unequal supports and a unified crown establish broader structural relationships. Horizontal plots use flush courtyards and finer dressed trims.

The shoulder lantern and its spotlight now leave with the lowest exterior wall during that storey's inspection. They return in City view and when the supporting lower masonry is visible. Impact smoke/debris use world-space rise and gravity after arrival; sparks eject toward the incoming side. Defeated rivals no longer receive invisible character/weapon animation updates.

Citizen batches now receive conservative local bounds after every pose update. Main and shadow cameras each perform their own native culling; no character geometry or instance packing is reduced. A full geometry audit checks **28,759,192 posed and cloth-morphed vertices**, across all six variants, three/twenty-storey Gothic fixtures, activity stations and inspection routes. Minimum envelope margin is 3.1584 local units; zero nonfinite/outside vertices or browser errors. Empty populations submit zero instances. Evidence: `artifacts/graphics-09-builder-02/culling/bounds-report.json`.

Eight paired full-game frames compare culling disabled/enabled with the same camera, time, lighting and scene. All eight encoded canvas PNGs are exactly identical. Submitted triangles fall from 16.97M to 12.16M in cyborg City view, 17.06M to 11.55M in flesh City view, and 15.58M to 8.71M in the tested lowest Streets views. World-view reductions are smaller. These are matching submission counters, not GPU frame-rate measurements. The same run verifies lantern visibility and spotlight suppression/restoration in both Gothic variants, with zero browser errors or remote requests. Evidence: `artifacts/graphics-09-builder-02/integration/integration-report.json`.

Stable destruction pools now submit only their occupied prefix. The real interaction factory passes 19 scenarios and 60,203 matrix/storage assertions, including full 512-slot capacity, actual overflow in both tree/rock directions, JSON reload, mode resets, 400 mixed footprint/tread slots and further recycling, and protected resources. A fresh world has zero active/submitted instances and zero visible pool meshes. Slot identities and storage capacity are preserved. Evidence: `artifacts/graphics-09-builder-02/pools`.

The flesh face now uses one continuous cheek/chin envelope and a localized jaw morph, with stronger torso and joint planes and a blended wrist/palm. Residents use fitted vest, collar, sleeve, scalp and hood forms. The head map also covers the jaw, leaving four active baked skin sets (torso/head/arm/leg); the preserved old jaw asset is unused. Character validation passes 1,179 compact-resident checks, minimum ceiling clearance 0.12736 local units, 475,500 skin samples and 3,641,760 cloth samples, with no nonfinite deformation or runtime errors. The complete six-variant contact audit passes 16 ground cases and 12 airborne ranged actions; largest contact error 0.01173 remains within the original bound. Seven close character views were inspected. Evidence: `artifacts/graphics-09-builder-02/{residents,characters,contact}`.
