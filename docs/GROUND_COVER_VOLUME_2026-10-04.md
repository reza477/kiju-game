## Implementation

`src/ground-cover-volume.js` builds its own deterministic cosmetic layer. It does not consume landscape generation randomness or register scenery/save IDs. Three broad low-poly plant masters share one opaque, untextured Lambert material. The final technical repair uses three global instanced drawing batches, replacing the original 48 spatial chunks while preserving their exact per-quality selection. They do not cast/receive shadows or participate in ray selection. Mild root-anchored sway uses the existing vegetation time uniform in the vertex shader. There is no per-plant or per-frame JavaScript update, matrix upload, extra render target or transparent foliage pass. Distance shrink and existing quality presets control its visual budget.

Placement excludes water/banks, roads, protected resource areas, the starting carrier footprint, steep slopes and original tree/rock cores. Existing scenery is not moved or replaced. Temporary placement objects are not retained in runtime diagnostics; shared geometry and instance buffers carry the drawable data. The only integration is five added lines in `src/landscape.js`: import, construction, adding the group, read-only statistics and quality forwarding. All ground/water material sources remain byte-identical.
