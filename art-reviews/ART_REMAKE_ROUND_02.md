## Concrete remaining visual defect

**1. Flesh has incoherent shredded body surfaces and unreadable anatomical masses.** The fresh art-remake-flesh/CRITIC-AFTER-desktop-close.png clearly shows irregular ribbon-like ridges and torn-looking projections across the back, flanks and limbs. The more organic brown hue and smoother normals make the material less metallic but do not produce the shoulder, torso, haunch and limb mass hierarchy expected of a sculpted living creature. The defect persists in native movement; it is not caused by one screenshot pose.

The next corrective attempt should address the visible geometry/form itself and verify it on the actual rig. Further global recoloring, normal averaging or uniform smoothing is unlikely by itself to reach the target. Preserve approved saddle attachment points, gameplay footprint and rig behavior while establishing broad coherent volumes with selective deliberate ridges. If the available mesh cannot support that outcome within these constraints, report that asset limitation honestly rather than describing the shape as fixed.

No additional runtime error was observed in the stated coverage. The zero-visual-error requirement is still not met.

## Ranked art-direction work before resubmission

These are score/completeness deficits, separate from the concrete defect above.

1. **Prioritize the hero creature silhouette and mass structure.** It occupies most of the screen in its faction, so unfinished anatomy outweighs small architecture improvements. The cyborg's more legible panels help, but its material separation can also be developed after the flesh form is solved.
2. **Give the world a deliberate medium-scale painted structure.** The prior scratch noise is gone, but normal and phone views now show broad almost blank beige ground. Introduce restrained land-following paint/value masses, connected habitat transitions and meaningful material variation. Do not restore the previous uniform high-frequency grain. Preserve terrain, resource and collision identities.
3. **Finish the moving cities as authored compositions.** The new arched crawler base is a useful direction. The visual center remains a large tiled rectangle with small repeated buildings; plain slab/pipe/track components and the minimally treated airship envelopes have not yet formed a distinctive new visual identity. Stronger structural joins, balanced value families and selective hero details matter more than adding uniform tiny props. Initial build-space emptiness is a gameplay requirement, not itself a defect.
4. **Keep the new consistent foliage/rock palette, then improve shape hierarchy.** Tiny triangular cover and repeated canopy clumps still dominate at close views. Aim for readable plant clusters and deliberate negative space rather than simply increasing scatter density.
5. **Retain the successful UI and evaluate phone readability at actual render scale.** The UI remains sharper than the 3D, especially landscape. Strong shape/value design should carry the scene before increasing render cost. No speedup or performance regression is inferred here.

## Ordinary polish opportunities

2. Weathered fabric, seams and attachments for both airship variants without making their clean envelope silhouettes busy; preserve exactly four upright balloons.

4. Close-view framing that reveals more limbs below the HUD, while preserving matched normal-City evidence.
5. More shaped tree shadows/contact at the base of clusters, avoiding a pasted-on appearance without adding expensive effects indiscriminately.
